# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## AI-Powered E-Commerce Platform for Flower & Cake Delivery

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2025-04-09  
**Author:** Product Team  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This document outlines the requirements for a full‑stack e‑commerce website specialized in flower and cake delivery. The platform differentiates itself through **advanced AI integration** that automates product sourcing, content generation, SEO optimization, competitor analysis, and multi‑channel management. It includes a **super admin panel** for global control and a **vendor panel** for order and earnings management.

The goal is to create a self‑improving e‑commerce system that reduces manual effort, boosts organic traffic, and maximizes vendor satisfaction.

---

## 2. Goals and Objectives

- **Automated Product Acquisition:** Fetch products from competitor websites, analyse them, and generate unique product listings using AI.
- **SEO Self‑Repair:** Continuously monitor and fix SEO issues (schema, meta tags, etc.) based on Google Search Console data.
- **AI‑Driven Content:** Automatically write SEO‑optimised blog posts and product descriptions targeting high‑demand keywords.
- **Full‑Funnel Management:** AI manages Google My Business, Merchant Center, and Ads to increase visibility and conversions.
- **Vendor Empowerment:** Provide vendors with real‑time order tracking, delivery status, and earnings analytics.
- **Super Admin Control:** Central dashboard to oversee all AI activities, product settings, and vendor performance.

---

## 3. Scope

### In Scope
- E‑commerce core (catalog, cart, checkout, payments, order management).
- Flower & cake specific attributes (delivery date/time, perishable handling).
- AI product import and image generation.
- SEO monitoring and auto‑fix module.
- Blog generation and keyword integration.
- AI‑powered management of Google services.
- Super admin and vendor dashboards.
- Integration with external APIs and MCP (Model Context Protocol) tools.

### Out of Scope
- Mobile native apps (responsive web only for initial release).
- Custom payment gateway development (use third‑party providers).
- Physical logistics management (delivery handled by vendors or third‑party couriers).

---

## 4. User Roles

1. **Customer** (implied – standard e‑commerce user)
   - Browse products, place orders, track delivery.
2. **Vendor**
   - Manage own products (if permitted).
   - View and update order status (accepted, preparing, out for delivery, delivered).
   - See earnings dashboard (daily, monthly, yearly).
3. **Super Admin**
   - Full system control.
   - Manage all products, vendors, AI settings, SEO, Google integrations.
   - Monitor AI logs and performance.
   - Approve/deny vendor product submissions.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### 5.1 E‑Commerce Core
- Product catalog with categories (Flowers, Cakes, Combos).
- Product variants (size, flavour, bouquet style).
- Shopping cart and secure checkout.
- Multiple payment methods (Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay).
- Order management (create, update, cancel, refund).
- Delivery slot selection (date and time).
- Email/SMS notifications for order status.
- Customer account with order history.

### 5.2 Product Management
- Manual product addition/editing by admin/vendor.
- Product attributes: name, description, price, images, stock, delivery availability.
- Bulk import/export via CSV.

### 5.3 AI Product Import & Generation
1. **Fetch & Download:** The system uses web scraping tools (e.g., Puppeteer, Scrapy) to fetch product data (name, price, description, images) from competitor websites. *Only publicly available data is scraped; compliance with robots.txt and legal guidelines is mandatory.*
2. **Analyse & Adopt:** AI (LLM + Computer Vision) analyses scraped products to extract key attributes and identify trends.
3. **Generate Unique Product:**
   - Use LLM to rewrite product titles, descriptions, and specifications ensuring originality.
   - Use generative image AI (e.g., DALL·E, Stable Diffusion) to create a **new, unique image inspired by the reference**, not a direct copy. The prompt is derived from the reference image’s semantic features.
4. **Vendor Assignment:** Auto‑assign the newly created product to a relevant vendor or keep in admin pool.

### 5.4 SEO Self‑Repair & Optimization
- **Google Search Console Integration:** Pull performance data, crawl errors, and indexing issues via API.
- **Automated Fixes:**
  - Generate missing meta titles, descriptions, alt tags.
  - Add structured data (schema.org for Product, LocalBusiness, FAQ).
  - Fix broken links, redirects, and 404s.
  - Optimise internal linking based on page authority.
- **Schema Markup:** Automatically generate JSON‑LD for products, reviews, breadcrumbs, and organisation.
- **On‑Page SEO:** AI checks keyword density, readability, and suggests improvements. Applies approved suggestions automatically.
- **Frequency:** Daily monitoring, immediate fixes for critical issues.

### 5.5 AI Content Generation (Blogs & SEO Pages)
- **Competitor Analysis:** Scrape competitor blogs for topics and keywords.
- **Keyword Mining:** Use Google Search Console + Keyword Planner + third‑party APIs (e.g., Ahrefs) to find high‑demand, low‑competition keywords.
- **Blog Writing:** LLM generates full articles (800–1500 words) with SEO structure (H1, H2, meta description, internal links).
- **Content Calendar:** AI schedules posts based on trending topics and seasonality (e.g., Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day).
- **Auto‑publish & Index:** Publish to blog, submit to Search Console for indexing.

### 5.6 AI Monitoring & Management
- **PageSpeed Insights:** Run daily audits, identify render‑blocking resources, image weight, etc. Auto‑compress images, enable lazy loading, and update critical CSS.
- **Google My Business (GMB):** Post updates, respond to reviews (template responses), upload product photos, and update business hours.
- **Google Merchant Center:** Automatically sync product feed, fix disapprovals, and optimise titles/descriptions for Shopping ads.
- **Google Ads:** Create and manage campaigns based on top‑converting keywords. AI adjusts bids and pauses underperforming ads.
- **Competitor Analysis:** Track competitor pricing, promotions, and new products. Alert admin and suggest price adjustments.

### 5.7 MCP & API Integrations
The system will leverage **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** tools and AI service APIs to achieve automation. A non‑exhaustive list of resources:

| Purpose                     | Tools / APIs                                                                 |
|-----------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Web scraping                | Puppeteer, Playwright, Scrapy, Apify                                        |
| Image generation            | OpenAI DALL·E, Stability AI, Midjourney API (if available)                  |
| Text generation / LLM       | OpenAI GPT‑4, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini                               |
| SEO data                    | Google Search Console API, Google PageSpeed Insights API, Ahrefs API, SEMrush |
| Google Business Profile     | Google My Business API                                                      |
| Google Merchant Center      | Content API for Shopping                                                    |
| Google Ads                  | Google Ads API                                                              |
| Structured data             | Schema.org vocabulary (manual generation via AI)                            |
| Monitoring & scheduling     | Cron jobs, Celery, Apache Airflow                                           |
| MCP servers (if applicable) | LangChain, Haystack, or custom MCP servers that expose tools for AI to call |

**Note:** The final MCP stack will be selected after a technical evaluation of reliability, cost, and compliance.

### 5.8 Super Admin Panel
- **Dashboard:** Overview of sales, traffic, AI actions, pending tasks.
- **AI Control Center:**
  - Enable/disable AI modules (product import, SEO repair, blog generation, etc.).
  - Set thresholds (e.g., minimum keyword volume, maximum scraping frequency).
  - Review AI‑generated products/blogs before publishing.
  - View logs of all AI activities.
- **Product Management:** Manual CRUD, approve/reject AI products, bulk edit.
- **Vendor Management:** Onboard/offboard vendors, set commission rates, assign products.
- **SEO Dashboard:** Integration with Search Console to display performance metrics.
- **Google Services:** Connect GMB, Merchant Center, Ads accounts. Monitor feed status.
- **Settings:** Store settings, payment gateways, shipping zones, tax rules.

### 5.9 Vendor Panel
- **Order Management:**
  - List of orders assigned to vendor (with customer details, delivery slot).
  - Update order status: Accepted → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered.
  - Cancellation requests.
- **Earnings Dashboard:**
  - Daily, monthly, yearly earnings with breakdown (commission, tips).
  - Payout history and pending payouts.
- **Product Management (if allowed):** Add/edit own products, set stock, view performance.
- **Notifications:** Real‑time alerts for new orders, status changes, low stock.

## 6. Technical Requirements

### 6.1 Architecture
- **Frontend:** React.js / Next.js (SSR for SEO).
- **Backend:** Node.js (Express) or Python (Django/FastAPI).
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (primary), Redis (caching, queues).
- **File Storage:** AWS S3 / Cloudinary for images.
- **Message Queue:** RabbitMQ or Kafka for asynchronous AI tasks.
- **AI Orchestration:** Custom service that connects to external AI APIs; uses MCP tool‑calling patterns.
- **Deployment:** Docker containers on Kubernetes (or managed PaaS like Heroku, Render).

### 6.2 AI/ML Models & Services
- **LLM:** GPT‑4 or Claude for text generation, rewriting, analysis.
- **Image Generation:** DALL·E 3 or Stable Diffusion XL.
- **Computer Vision:** Google Cloud Vision or AWS Rekognition for analysing reference images.
- **Speech‑to‑Text (optional):** For voice search SEO (future).

### 6.3 External APIs
- Google APIs (Search Console, PageSpeed, GMB, Merchant Center, Ads).
- Payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal).
- Email/SMS (SendGrid, Twilio).
- Web scraping proxies (Bright Data, ScraperAPI) to avoid IP bans.

### 6.4 Security
- HTTPS everywhere.
- OAuth2 for Google service authentication.
- Role‑based access control (RBAC).
- Data encryption at rest and in transit.
- GDPR compliance (if serving EU customers) – user data export/deletion.

### 6.5 Performance
- CDN for static assets.
- Image compression (WebP).
- Lazy loading, code splitting.
- Server response < 200ms for standard pages.

## 7. Non‑Functional Requirements

- **Scalability:** Handle up to 10,000 concurrent users; auto‑scale AI workers.
- **Reliability:** 99.9% uptime for core e‑commerce functions; AI modules can degrade gracefully.
- **Maintainability:** Modular microservices; comprehensive logging and monitoring (ELK stack, Prometheus).
- **Usability:** Admin and vendor dashboards must be intuitive with minimal training.
- **Compliance:** Respect robots.txt, copyright laws, and Google’s terms of service.

---

## 8. Success Metrics

- **SEO:** Increase organic traffic by 50% in 6 months.
- **AI Product Import:** At least 100 new unique products added per week with 90% accuracy.
- **Vendor Satisfaction:** Vendor NPS > 60.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Reduce manual SEO work by 80%.
- **Google Ads ROAS:** Achieve > 4x return on ad spend.

---

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Legal issues from scraping | Use allowed sources, respect robots.txt, add disclaimer, focus on public data. |
| AI‑generated content quality | Human review for critical pages; continuous model fine‑tuning. |
| API cost overruns | Set monthly budgets, use caching, optimise prompts. |
| Google account suspension | Follow Google policies strictly; avoid black‑hat SEO tactics. |
| Vendor data privacy | Isolate vendor data; only show necessary customer info to vendors. |
