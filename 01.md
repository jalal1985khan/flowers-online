1. Executive Summary

This product is a full-stack, multi-vendor ecommerce platform for same-day and scheduled flower and cake delivery, combined with an AI-driven commerce and growth operating system.

The platform has three connected surfaces:

Customer storefront — browse, personalize, buy, schedule delivery, track orders and receive local delivery options.

Vendor portal — accept/manage orders, update fulfillment, manage availability and inventory, and view earnings.

Super Admin + AI Growth Control Center — operate the marketplace and continuously improve products, SEO, content, site performance, competitor intelligence, Google Business Profile, Google Merchant Center and Google Ads.

The differentiator is an AI Commerce Growth Engine that continuously observes the store, gathers permitted market intelligence, detects opportunities, proposes changes, executes approved changes, measures results, and learns from outcomes.

The system must not be designed around blindly copying competitors. Product ingestion must only use lawful/authorized sources such as merchant-provided catalogs, supplier feeds, licensed APIs, affiliate/product feeds, or public information where collection is permitted by applicable law and the source's terms. Competitor references are used to identify market patterns and generate original differentiated products and content.

For generative content, AI may accelerate research, drafting, product merchandising and image creation, but must not create large volumes of low-value pages merely to manipulate search rankings. Google's guidance states that AI-generated content becomes problematic when it is used to generate many pages without adding value, so the platform must enforce quality gates, originality, topical usefulness and human/business approval policies.

2. Product Vision

Vision

Build an ecommerce engine where a business can launch and operate a flower/cake delivery marketplace while an AI operations layer continuously answers:

What is missing, what should be improved, what should be added, what is changing in the market, and which action is most likely to increase profitable orders?

Product Principles

AI-first, not AI-only: AI proposes and executes bounded actions; deterministic systems validate them.

Human-governed automation: every high-impact action has configurable approval requirements.

Originality over duplication: competitor research informs positioning; it does not authorize copying.

Evidence-backed recommendations: every recommendation includes the observed signal, expected impact, confidence and affected entities.

Rollback everything: every automated write must have a diff, actor, timestamp and rollback path.

Business outcomes over vanity metrics: optimize contribution margin, conversion, delivery success, repeat purchase and qualified organic traffic.

Local-intent first: delivery area, inventory availability, delivery slot and fulfillment capability are first-class entities.

Composable integrations: Google APIs and MCP servers are integration adapters, not the business logic itself.

3. Goals and Non-Goals

3.1 Goals

Launch a fast SEO-friendly flower and cake storefront.

Support multiple vendors and location-aware fulfillment.

Import authorized product/catalog feeds and normalize them.

Use AI to create differentiated product concepts, descriptions, attributes, SEO metadata and original image assets.

Continuously audit technical SEO and ecommerce structured data.

Integrate Google Search Console and GA4 for performance intelligence.

Monitor page speed/Core Web Vitals.

Manage Google Business Profile locations.

Manage Google Merchant Center product presence.

Integrate Google Ads for campaign intelligence and controlled optimization.

Provide an AI growth dashboard with alerts, recommendations, experiments and approvals.

Provide strong auditability, permissions and rollback.

Expose the internal platform capabilities as MCP tools where useful.

3.2 Non-Goals for MVP

Autonomous uncontrolled bidding/spending in Google Ads.

Fully autonomous competitor scraping without source authorization.

Exact recreation of a competitor's copyrighted product photography or brand assets.

Replacing accounting/ERP/payroll.

Building an in-house payment processor.

Guaranteeing search ranking improvements.

10.3 Image Policy

The reference image can be used for analysis where permission allows.

The default platform behavior should be:

do not republish the source image;

generate an original image/variation;

maintain provenance;

run brand/logo/person/IP checks;

allow manual designer override.

The user-facing claim should be “AI-generated original product image based on approved references,” not “exact copy,” unless the business owns or licenses the source asset.

19. MCP Strategy

19.1 MCP Role in This Product

MCP should be used as a tool interoperability layer for the AI agent system, not as the primary source of business rules.

Architecture:

AI Orchestrator -> Policy Layer -> MCP Tool Router -> Integration Adapters -> External APIs

MCP provides tools, resources and prompts. Its architecture separates the host/client from specialized servers and provides security boundaries and capability negotiation.

39. Major Risks

Risk: Copyright/IP infringement

Mitigation: provenance, licensed/authorized sources, original generation, legal review, no exact image cloning.

Risk: Search spam

Mitigation: human-value thresholds, page creation limits, originality checks, no keyword-only page generation.

Risk: Bad AI changes

Mitigation: staging, approvals, validation, rollback, bounded autonomy.

Risk: Google API quota exhaustion

Mitigation: queues, caching, incremental syncs, backoff and quota dashboards.

Risk: OAuth credential compromise

Mitigation: encryption, secret manager, minimal scopes, token rotation and reauthorization.

Risk: Prompt injection from competitor pages

Mitigation: sandboxing, sanitization, treating all fetched content as untrusted.

Risk: AI-generated visual sameness

Mitigation: creative briefs, similarity thresholds, asset review and a proprietary visual style guide.

40. Critical Product Decisions

Use direct Google APIs as the source of truth.

Use MCP as a standardized AI tool layer rather than as the underlying integration database.

Build an internal MCP server for the platform.

Do not rely on scraping as the default catalog acquisition mechanism.

Make product imports permission-aware.

Generate differentiated products, not replicas.

Make all high-impact AI writes policy-controlled.

Store complete before/after state for every automated change.

Treat SEO as a continuous optimization loop, not a one-time audit.

Optimize for revenue and delivery success, not only rankings.

41. Current Integration Research Notes

Google Search Console

Official REST API provides property, sitemap, search analytics and URL inspection access. API quotas apply, including site/user/project limits and URL inspection limits.

Google Analytics

GA4 Data API supports reporting and ecommerce metrics. Google also provides an experimental official Analytics MCP server that is read-only.

Google Business Profile

Official APIs support multi-location business profile management, reviews, posts and notifications.

Google Merchant Center

Merchant API is the current API surface, with product/data-source/promotions/reports functionality and explicit quotas. OAuth is appropriate for third-party SaaS management; service accounts are suitable for own-business automation.

Google Ads

Google Ads API requires a developer token and OAuth 2.0. Official client libraries exist for several languages. Use direct API access for deterministic production workflows.

PageSpeed

PageSpeed Insights API supplies Lighthouse and CrUX-related performance information and recommendations. Use it as the performance evidence layer.

MCP

MCP provides standardized host/client/server architecture with tools, resources and prompts. Official Google MCP availability varies by product. The platform should not equate “MCP exists” with “safe for autonomous production writes.”

43. Final Product Definition

The product should be treated as:

A multi-vendor flower & cake commerce platform with an AI operating system for catalog creation, SEO, content, local discovery, product visibility, performance and advertising.

The customer-facing commerce system creates revenue.
The vendor system creates supply and operational reliability.
The AI growth system continuously improves discoverability and merchandising.
